A BIT MORE CONTEXT

ABOUT

My background is in materials science and nanotechnology, but I tend to gravitate towards problems that sit somewhere between practical engineering and analysis. I like getting close enough to a process to understand what it is actually doing, then using measurements, code and models to work out why.

I generally prefer testing an assumption to arguing about it. Much of the work I enjoy starts with something behaving strangely and ends with enough data to explain what was going on. I am equally comfortable digging through results in Python as I am doing the experimental work needed to generate them.

Outside of engineering, I spend a fairly unreasonable amount of time tinkering with computers, programming, games and whatever technical rabbit hole happens to look interesting that week.
